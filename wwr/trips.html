Featured Trips

Big Mallard

Difficulty: Intermediate

Duration: Full Day (8 hours)

Distance: 12 miles

Experience the rush of Big Mallard, one of our most popular trips! This full-day adventure takes you through some of the most exciting rapids in the region. Perfect for those looking for a genuine thrill while still enjoying scenic views. Our expert guides will coach you through each rapid, making it an unforgettable experience.

Los Dientes del Diablo

Difficulty: Advanced

Duration: Full Day (8 hours)

Distance: 15 miles

"The Devil's Teeth" lives up to its name with challenging rapids and stunning canyon views. This trip is designed for experienced rafters seeking an adrenaline-pumping adventure. Navigate through technical whitewater sections, explore hidden side canyons, and camp under the stars. This is not for the faint of heart!

Half Day Adventure

Difficulty: Beginner

Duration: Half Day (4 hours)

Distance: 6 miles

Perfect for families and first-time rafters! The Half Day Adventure offers gentle rapids, beautiful scenery, and plenty of time to enjoy nature. Ideal for introducing loved ones to the thrill of whitewater rafting without overwhelming them. Our guides make sure everyone feels confident and safe throughout the journey.

Rapid Rush

Difficulty: Intermediate to Advanced

Duration: Full Day (8 hours)

Distance: 14 miles

Get your adrenaline pumping on the Rapid Rush trip! This action-packed adventure features back-to-back rapids, stunning drops, and breathtaking canyon walls. Great for experienced paddlers or brave intermediates looking to push their limits. Lunch included with this trip!

Thunder Run

Difficulty: Advanced

Duration: Multi-Day (3 days)

Distance: 30 miles

The ultimate rafting experience! Thunder Run is a three-day expedition through pristine wilderness, featuring world-class rapids, stunning camp sites, and unforgettable sunsets. This immersive adventure is perfect for those seeking to disconnect and reconnect with nature. All meals and camping equipment provided.
