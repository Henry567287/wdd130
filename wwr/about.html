About Us-Exciting Expeditions

RiverRush Rafting delivers thrilling river experiences with safety, expert guides, and unforgettable outdoor adventures for every paddler. Founded by river guides who love the rush of whitewater, RiverRush began as a small local outfitter and grew into a trusted rafting company known for its passion and safety.

History

Exciting Expeditions was founded in 1998 by a group of passionate river guides who wanted to share their love for whitewater adventures with others. It has since become a leading name in the rafting industry, known for its commitment to safety and unforgettable experiences.
